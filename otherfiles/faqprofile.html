FAQs

What is Joury?

Joury is an application designed to help users document their thoughts, plans, and experiences in an organized and secure way.

Is the journal app free to use?

Yes, our journal app is free for all users with a lot of features and advanced customization options.

How do I create an account?

Simply click the “Sign Up” button on the menu bar and register by entering your full name, birth of date, email, and password.

Can I access my journal offline?

Yes, you can write and save entries offline, and they will sync automatically once you are connected to the internet.

How do I report a bug or give feedback?

Simply click the “Contact Us” button on the menu bar and fill in the form to report a bug or give feedback.
